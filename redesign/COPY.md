## 4. How it works

**Section heading:** `From brief to live calls in four steps`

**Step 1 — Describe the caller experience.**
Write a plain-English brief: who calls, what the agent should accomplish, and what it must never say. No prompt engineering, no flowchart wiring.

**Step 2 — Review the Agent Spec.**
VoiceForge compiles your brief into Agent Spec JSON — voice, scripts, tools, guardrails, and compliance rules in one auditable, versioned contract.

**Step 3 — Run the mock call lab.**
Place simulated calls against the spec. Listen to recordings, inspect transcripts, and fix failure modes before a real number ever rings.

**Step 4 — Publish, monitor, improve.**
Deploy through your provider adapter, watch live analytics, and iterate by versioning the spec. Every change is tracked and reversible.

## 5. Features grid

**Section heading:** `Everything between the idea and the call`

**Feature 1 — Agent Spec JSON**
Every agent compiles to a single versioned contract: persona, scripts, tools, guardrails. Review it, diff it, roll it back.

**Feature 2 — Compliance gates**
Consent capture, DNC screening, AI disclosure, and permitted call windows are enforced before any outbound call dials.

**Feature 3 — Provider adapters**
One spec, many carriers. Deploy the same agent to Vapi, Retell, or Twilio without rewriting a line.

**Feature 4 — Multi-tenant workspaces**
Isolate clients, campaigns, and credentials per workspace, with role-based access for your whole team.

**Feature 5 — White-label delivery**
Ship client-facing dashboards under your brand — your domain, your logo, your reports, your margins.

**Feature 6 — Transcripts & analytics**
Every call lands with a searchable transcript, outcome tags, and cost and conversion analytics you can act on.
